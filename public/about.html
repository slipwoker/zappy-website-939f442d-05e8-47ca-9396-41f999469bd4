About Daria

Physicist.
Author.
Storyteller.

Daria holds a PhD in physics and has spent her career dismantling the wall between the laboratory and the library. She is the author of acclaimed works that translate the mathematics of the universe into language that moves, provokes, and inspires — reaching readers who never thought science could speak to them.

As a public speaker and podcast host, she brings the same precision and wonder to live audiences and audio listeners worldwide, turning complexity into compelling public thought. Her work sits at the rare intersection where rigorous science meets genuine literary craft.

What I Stand For

These are not abstract principles. They are the live wires running through every lecture, every page, every episode — the commitments that shape how I think, create, and connect.

01

Intellectual Rigor

Precision is not the enemy of wonder — it is its foundation. Every claim I make is grounded in evidence, every metaphor tested against the physics it represents. I believe that the most honest thing a scientist can do is resist the comfortable shortcut, sit with complexity, and still find a way to make it sing. Rigor is not coldness. It is the deepest form of respect for the audience.

Science · Integrity · Depth
02

Narrative Accessibility

A PhD should not be a barrier — it should be a bridge. I write and speak to close the gap between the laboratory and the living room, translating equations into emotions without losing the truth inside them. Great science storytelling is an act of radical generosity.

Storytelling · Clarity · Empathy
03

Interdisciplinary Curiosity

The most interesting questions live at the borders — between physics and philosophy, between a novel and a research paper, between a podcast studio and a lecture hall. I refuse to stay in one lane. Curiosity is not a distraction; it is the method.

Exploration · Connection · Wonder
